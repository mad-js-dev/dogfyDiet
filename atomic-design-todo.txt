## 🎨 Design Principles

### Atomic Design Guidelines
- **Single Responsibility**: Each component has one clear purpose
- **Composition Ready**: Components designed to combine into larger molecules
- **Consistent API**: Similar props and events across components
- **Material Design 3**: Follows Google's Material Design system
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance built-in
- **Theme Support**: Automatic light/dark mode switching
- **Responsive**: Mobile-first approach with breakpoints
